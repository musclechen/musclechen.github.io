Data Deletion Request

Players can request deletion of information associated with their use of Tasty Match where applicable.

To request deletion of information related to Tasty Match, email us and include enough device information for us to understand your request.

How to Request Deletion

Please email cyfckl2008@gmail.com with the subject line:

Data Deletion Request - Tasty Match

Please Include

  • Your device type and operating system version
  • Your app version, if available
  • A brief description of the data you want deleted

Important Note

If the App stores progress only on your device, deleting the App may remove local data. Some data collected by third-party services, such as advertising or analytics providers, may need to be managed through those providers or through your device settings.
