Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Kitchen Match handles information related to your use of the game.

Last updated: May 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kitchen Match ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile game.

Kitchen Match does not require players to create an account or submit direct personal information such as name, phone number, or address to start playing.

1. Information We Collect

We do not directly collect personal information such as your name, email address, address, or payment information. However, limited information may be collected automatically by the App, Apple services, Unity, or third-party advertising services.

This may include:

  • Device identifiers, including advertising identifiers where permitted by your device settings
  • Advertising data
  • App usage and interaction data
  • Crash data and performance data
  • General device information, such as device type, operating system version, and language settings

2. Third-Party Advertising

Kitchen Match may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob, to display ads in the App.

These services may collect and process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage data, and other technical information to provide, limit, measure, and improve advertisements. Depending on your privacy choices and device settings, ads may be personalized or non-personalized.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Google AdMob information: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

3. App Tracking Transparency and Personalized Ads

On iOS devices, the App may request permission to allow tracking through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency framework. If you allow tracking, third-party advertising partners may use your advertising identifier to provide personalized ads and measure performance.

If you do not allow tracking, you may still see ads, but they may be less relevant and based on contextual information rather than cross-app tracking.

  • iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  • iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising

4. How We Use Information

Information collected automatically may be used to:

  • Display advertisements in the App
  • Measure and improve advertising performance
  • Maintain and improve App functionality
  • Diagnose crashes, bugs, and performance issues
  • Improve user experience and game stability
  • Comply with legal, platform, and security requirements

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by third-party service providers, such as advertising and analytics providers, only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy and subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Children’s Privacy

Kitchen Match is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to address the issue where applicable.

7. Data Retention

We do not maintain user accounts or directly store personal user profiles. Information collected by third-party services is retained according to those services’ own data retention policies.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information associated with the App. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. User Choices

You can control certain data collection and advertising preferences through your device settings, including tracking permissions and personalized advertising settings.

You may also delete the App from your device at any time to stop further data collection by the App.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
